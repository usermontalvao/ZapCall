# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

In short: be respectful and constructive; harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated; assume good faith and disagree on the merits. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that do not follow these standards, and may ban repeat offenders.

Report unacceptable behaviour through the contact listed on the repository profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
